Support & Privacy Policy

Support

Questions, bugs or purchase problems? Write to Oleksandrfedykovych@gmail.com. Please mention your device and iOS version. I usually reply within a few days.

Lost your purchase on a new device? Open the chapter list and tap “Restore purchases”.

Privacy Policy

Aurora does not collect, store or share any personal data. The game has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. It works fully offline.

Your game progress is saved only on your device. Deleting the app deletes it.

The one-time purchase “Full Story” is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. The developer does not receive your payment details.

The game is not directed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone.

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page.
