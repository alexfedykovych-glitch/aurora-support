If you reinstall the game or switch devices, open Shop → Crystals and tap Restore Purchases to get back the Nova ship. Crystal packs are consumable and are stored on your device.

Privacy Policy

Effective: November 19, 2026 · Developer: Oleksandr Fedykovych

Exodus: Planet Nine does not collect, store on servers, share or sell any personal data.

  • No accounts. The game has no sign-in and does not ask for your name, email or any other personal information.
  • Local storage only. Your progress, settings and in-game currency are saved only on your device. Deleting the app deletes this data.
  • No ads, analytics or tracking. The game contains no advertising, no third-party analytics and does not track you across apps or websites.
  • Purchases. In-app purchases are processed by Apple. We do not receive your payment details. Apple’s privacy policy applies to payments.
  • Children. The game does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children.

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page. Contact: Oleksandrfedykovych@gmail.com.
